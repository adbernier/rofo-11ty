# San Francisco Canonical Representative Building Collection

Sprint W0 editorial foundation.

This document defines Rofo's canonical representative building collection for San Francisco. It is an editorial source of truth, not a migration script, availability dataset, listings feed, or automatic replacement for the current building database.

Representative buildings are selected because they help explain how commercial real estate works across San Francisco's major business districts. A building belongs here when studying it helps a business understand district character, tenant fit, building format, tradeoffs, and nearby alternatives.

## Editorial Methodology

Selection was based on current public information and editorial judgment, using:

- public owner, developer, and building websites
- public-agency planning and development materials
- broker and leasing pages where useful
- architecture references
- recent market reporting
- existing Rofo representative-building inventory only for migration comparison

Selection was not based on live availability. A building may be representative even when no space is available. Conversely, a building with available space may be excluded if it does not teach the market well.

The collection prioritizes:

- district representation
- market significance
- business diversity
- tenant recognition where relevant
- architectural or historical identity
- leasing relevance
- building-era diversity
- educational value

## Relationship Observations

- Businesses comparing 555 California usually should also compare 101 California, 345 California, One Bush, One Sansome, and Transamerica Pyramid Center.
- Businesses comparing Transamerica Pyramid Center should also evaluate One Maritime Plaza, Levi's Plaza, and 555 California depending on whether image, neighborhood character, or institutional scale matters most.
- Businesses comparing Salesforce Tower should also compare 181 Fremont, 303 Second, 680 Folsom, and 425 Market.
- Businesses comparing 650 Townsend or 888 Brannan should also compare 600 Townsend, 2 Henry Adams, 808 Brannan, and 680 Folsom.
- Businesses exploring Mission Bay should compare Uber Mission Bay, The Exchange, Alexandria Center buildings, 500/550 Terry Francois, and Dogpatch Power Station projects.
- Businesses comparing Dogpatch and Mission Bay should study Pier 70 Building 12, Power Station - Station A, the UCSF Life Sciences Building, 1700 Owens, and 550 Terry Francois.
- Businesses comparing Design District and Showplace Square should compare Showplace, Galleria, 2 Henry Adams, 808 Brannan, 460 Townsend, and 650 Townsend.
- Businesses considering South Beach should compare 188 Spear, The Spear, 303 Second, Rincon Center, and China Basin / 185 Berry.
- Businesses choosing Potrero Hill should compare 99 Rhode Island, 1000 16th, 150 Hooper, 350 Rhode Island, and Dogpatch/Showplace alternatives.
- Businesses exploring the Mission should compare the Armory, Heath Ceramics, 1850 Bryant, Mission Creative, and nearby Potrero/Showplace buildings rather than treating the district like a conventional office market.
