# San Francisco Industrial Geography Foundation

Status: Canonical geography approved; evidence and public publishing deferred

## Purpose

This foundation reconciles San Francisco's Industrial/Flex decision geography before Commercial Market Evidence, Representative Building Intelligence, recommendations, or public publishing expand.

It implements the hierarchy:

```text
San Francisco Market
├── Bayview Industrial — canonical occupier decision district
│   ├── Northern Gateway / Bayview Industrial Triangle — internal subdistrict
│   ├── India Basin / Oakinba / SF Market Area — internal subdistrict
│   ├── South Basin — internal subdistrict
│   └── Southern Waterfront / Piers 80–96 — specialized operating area, not a conventional leasing district
├── Central Waterfront — canonical occupier decision district
│   └── Central Waterfront Core PDR — internal industrial core
├── Dogpatch — existing canonical mixed-use/adaptive district
├── Showplace Square — canonical knowledge owner for the overlapping design-trade geography
│   └── Design District — preserved compatibility district identity and URL pending a separate migration decision
├── Potrero Hill — existing canonical district with industrial fit bounded to eastern/base PDR edges
└── Mission District — existing canonical district with industrial fit bounded to Northeast Mission PDR
```

Internal subdistricts and specialized operating areas are structured context on their owning Knowledge Graph district. They are not peer canonical districts, recommendation targets, CME denominators, or public-page instructions.

## Canonical Decisions

### Bayview Industrial

Bayview Industrial is distinct from the broader residential and mixed-use Bayview neighborhood. It is the San Francisco decision district for city-serving warehouse, distribution, wholesale food, contractor and service-industrial operations, fleets, light manufacturing, production, and operational flex.

The district is intentionally not described as uniformly suitable. Loading, truck access, fleet and yard needs, power, permitted use, environmental history, shoreline and flood conditions, neighborhood adjacency, and property constraints remain validation questions.

### Central Waterfront and Dogpatch

Central Waterfront is the more operational protected PDR district for production, fabrication, maker, service-industrial, practical flex, product development, prototyping, and maritime-support uses. Dogpatch remains the existing mixed-use and adaptive northern identity with Mission Bay-adjacent office/R&D relevance. Neither replaces the other.

### Southern Waterfront

Southern Waterfront / Piers 80–96 is nested specialized context. It describes Port-jurisdiction maritime, cargo, rail, bulk-material, vehicle-processing, warehousing, and heavy-commercial activity. It is not a conventional tenant-search district and should not generate ordinary Building Profile or recommendation behavior without a specialized evidence sprint.

### Showplace Square and Design District

Showplace Square owns future shared knowledge for the substantially overlapping showroom, design-trade, creative-production, and customer-facing PDR geography. Design District remains a first-class compatibility record because consolidation would require a separate recommendation, CME, building-ownership, public URL, and redirect migration. New evidence should not treat the two as unrelated peer markets.

### Potrero Hill and Mission District

Potrero Hill industrial relevance is limited to its eastern/base areas, production-adjacent blocks, and Showplace/Potrero edge. Mission District industrial relevance is limited to Northeast Mission PDR and the 16th/17th Street production/service-industrial edge. Their broader residential, retail, office, and community identities remain intact.

## Follow-On Evidence Work

### Ready

- American Industrial Center: evaluate reassignment or shared evidence between Dogpatch and Central Waterfront without changing its public URL.
- Existing Pier 70 records: review which explain Dogpatch transition and which explain Central Waterfront production/maritime context.
- Existing Showplace Square and Design District collections: prepare a non-destructive ownership map under Showplace Square before any record migration.

### Researchable

- The SF Market: wholesale food, warehouse, and distribution campus evidence for Bayview Industrial.
- SF Gateway: modern multistory PDR and city-serving logistics evidence for Bayview Industrial.
- Bayview warehouse/service-industrial properties that explain small-bay, contractor, fleet, distribution, and light-manufacturing operating models.
- Food-production and food-logistics environments near the SF Market.
- Central Waterfront production, fabrication, maker, and practical flex buildings beyond the existing Dogpatch collection.

### Specialized / Not Ordinary Building Profile Work

- Piers 80–96 and associated Port terminals: environment-level evidence for maritime, cargo, rail, bulk materials, vehicle processing, and heavy commercial activity.
- Southern Waterfront shoreline, resilience, and Port-jurisdiction validation context.

### Blocked

- Ordinary tenant or building recommendations for Port infrastructure until tenancy, jurisdiction, access, and evidence rules are explicitly designed.
- Design District URL consolidation until a dedicated compatibility migration covers recommendations, CME, buildings, public routes, redirects, and analytics.
- Public Bayview Industrial or Central Waterfront pages until their CME and representative-environment foundations pass review.

## Recommendation Implications

The current SF Office recommendation model remains unchanged. Future Industrial/Flex QA should test:

- city-serving warehouse/distribution -> Bayview Industrial
- urban production/prototyping -> Central Waterfront
- adaptive mixed-use office/R&D -> Dogpatch
- showroom/design-trade -> Showplace Square
- smaller Northeast Mission production -> Mission District as a bounded secondary path
- larger regional logistics -> Bayview compared with Hayward or Union City rather than assumed to fit inside San Francisco
- Port-dependent cargo or maritime need -> specialized investigation, not a normal district shortlist

## Publishing Gate

Canonical geography completion authorizes evidence work, not public publishing. Public district pages, comparisons, and an Industrial/Flex hub should wait for the follow-on evidence sprint.
