# Location Brief v2 — Universal Intelligence Projection

## Audit finding

Before Sprint 7, the private v2 Brief had a strong certified SF experience: a concise search summary, peer locations, reasons, tradeoffs, comparison, representative environments, and a property-stage next action. The weak point was the `INVESTIGATE` path. Outside reviewed SF intelligence it could render little or no useful guidance, while its section names and fallback copy were still shaped around Office and local comparison.

The existing `Your search` summary remains the Business Profile. A separate “What Rofo understands” card would duplicate it, so Sprint 7 does not add one.

## Adaptive hierarchy

Both states now share: Location Brief hero, `Your search`, `What matters for this search`, `What we'll investigate next`, and the existing next action.

Certified SF inserts peer `Locations worth investigating`, reasons, tradeoffs, comparison, Access, and representative environments between the two universal layers. A non-certified market instead keeps the selected market as the starting point, suppresses any local resolver output, and explains that local comparison requires research.

Only Requirement-activated dimensions are normally shown. When a Requirement is too sparse to activate a dimension, the projection uses a bounded two-dimension space-type baseline rather than an empty Brief or a complete generic checklist.

## Unknowns and claims

Missing Requirement signals remain in the projection and commercial context but are not rendered as assumed answers. Property capability, local access, economics, availability, and use compatibility remain investigation topics. The universal layer cannot emit district rankings, local characteristics, market statistics, or building claims.

## Next action audit

The existing CTA remains the current product boundary. Public owner-authorized SF Office Briefs can continue to Property Requirement; unsupported combinations retain the existing gated/disabled fallback. Copy continues to describe progression toward compatible spaces, but expanding property-stage continuation beyond its current supported contract is deferred rather than implied by this sprint.

## Commercial handoff

The existing vNext commercial context now includes a bounded structured universal projection: foundation IDs, important dimension labels, investigation topics, missing Requirement signals, and the local-evidence boundary code. It contains no new PII and does not alter lead creation, qualification, routing, OfficeFinder, or notification semantics.

## Requirement gaps for Sprint 8

High value: Industrial/Flex applicability and use mix; truck/vehicle needs; loading form; office-production ratio; customer-facing priority; Retail frontage/signage and delivery needs; Office collaboration/private-work mix and growth horizon.

Nice to have: detailed adjacency preferences, floorplate preference, detailed customer-entry character, and more precise adaptability preferences.

Excessive/deferred: conference-room programming, exhaustive building specifications, exact column grids/floor loads before property investigation, and long amenity wishlists.
