# Industrial/Flex Market Pack Proof

## Executive Verdict

**GO.** Sacramento's certified Industrial/Flex behavior can be reproduced from a small declarative Market Pack plus shared evaluation logic. A default-off, test-only path reproduced the current Requirement projection, abstention, candidate evaluation, ordering, shortlist, evidence references, representative projection, and Location Brief recommendation snapshot across 18 decision cases. No production path imports the proof.

This does not prove that every Industrial/Flex market fits one schema. It proves the narrower point the sprint was designed to test: most of Sacramento's adapter and composition code is market knowledge expressed procedurally. The remaining algorithm is reusable. Sacramento's reviewed evidence foundation remains canonical and is referenced rather than copied into the pack.

The next prudent step is a second controlled conversion of a contrasting existing market, not a new-market launch and not a wholesale refactor. Phoenix is the best proof case because its conventional, advanced-operations, and mixed operating environments should expose whether the contract handles more than Sacramento's two-peer universe.

## Sacramento Today

The certified path currently depends on:

- `_data/sacramentoIndustrialFlexEvidenceFoundation.js`: 233 lines of reviewed geography, evidence, traits, distinctions, representatives, provenance, exclusions, and evidence boundaries. This is predominantly market knowledge and should remain canonical.
- `lib/requirements/requirement-to-sacramento-industrial-flex-recommendation.js`: 70 lines covering Sacramento membership, aliases, intent signals, unsupported boundaries, and abstention.
- `lib/recommendations/sacramento-industrial-flex-location-composition.js`: 67 lines covering trait activation, decisive traits, fit bands, candidate comparison, mixed handling, ordering, and shortlist construction.
- `lib/recommendations/private-recommendation-readiness.js`: a Sacramento universe wrapper plus Sacramento branches for membership, composition, one-peer readiness, and product-response boundary language.
- activation, public-eligibility, evidence, recommendation, certification, and rendering QA that establish default-deny operation and customer-visible behavior.

The evidence foundation is already data-like. The main repetition is that the adapter and composer combine market facts with algorithms that closely resemble the corresponding San Diego, North Orange County, Phoenix, and Indianapolis paths.

## Market Knowledge vs Market Software

### Market knowledge

The following belong to Sacramento and were represented declaratively in the proof:

- City of Sacramento ownership, state aliases, property type, and the Sacramento Metro exclusion.
- The two canonical peers: Power Inn Industrial and Northgate / North Market Industrial.
- Component and entry aliases, including Florin-Perkins and SCI/Ramona ownership under Power Inn.
- Industrial and Flex activity signals and Sacramento's reviewed text signals.
- Unsupported regional, specialized-use, property-capability, access, and conflicting-use boundaries, including their exact existing customer explanations.
- Candidate Industrial/Flex fit bands.
- Reviewed trait labels and the rules connecting Requirement signals to those traits.
- The circumstances in which manufacturing, lighter warehouse, lighter Flex, or service-industrial traits become decisive.
- The certification identifier and activation eligibility. These are descriptive in the proof; they do not activate anything.

### Shared product logic

The proof evaluator is unaware of Sacramento names or geography once the pack is supplied. Shared behavior includes:

- normalizing canonical Requirement inputs;
- resolving municipality, property type, and candidate membership from configured ownership;
- counting Industrial and Flex signals and resolving industrial-led, flex-led, mixed, or unresolved intent;
- applying abstention rules in the existing precedence order;
- activating reviewed traits from Requirement facts;
- comparing activated traits with the canonical evidence foundation;
- preserving required/preferred criteria without using them as unsupported geography facts;
- enforcing decisive-trait boundaries;
- sorting by reviewed fit, matched traits, and canonical geography ID;
- limiting the shortlist to two;
- intersecting independently supported Industrial and Flex results for mixed intent; and
- projecting evidence, representatives, unknowns, comparison context, and Location Brief explanation data.

### Market-specific software

Today, the Sacramento adapter and composer encode the preceding market facts directly in JavaScript and repeat the shared algorithm. The readiness engine also contains Sacramento-named dispatch and response branches. Those branches are not unique product behavior; they are a consequence of the current per-market module arrangement.

Genuine Sacramento-specific behavior remains the bounded City ownership, aliases, candidate set, evidence, trait relationships, abstention boundaries, and customer-facing scope language. Those belong in governed data even when the evaluator is shared.

## Minimum Market Pack

The proof needed only these fields:

1. **Market identity:** market ID, label, municipality, accepted state aliases, property type, boundary label, and canonical evidence-foundation version.
2. **Membership:** accepted and rejected market aliases, canonical candidate ownership for component aliases, and stable reason codes.
3. **Intent signals:** Industrial activities, Flex activities, reviewed text signals, signal weight, and dominance rule.
4. **Abstention knowledge:** outside-scope, specialized-use, exact-capability, and decisive-access patterns; conflicting activity groups; existing bounded explanations.
5. **Composition knowledge:** ordered candidate IDs, fit bands by resolved model, customer labels for reviewed traits, derived signals, signal-to-trait rules, and decisive-trait rules.
6. **Certification metadata:** certification state, activation key, activation eligibility, and the explicit test-only proof mode.

The pack does not duplicate geography labels, paths, provenance, representative buildings, strengths, tradeoffs, or property-verification limits. Those continue to come from the existing evidence foundation. It also does not contain presentation templates, Location Brief prose, runtime switches, or market-specific renderer code.

## Shared Logic

The test-only evaluator consumes the pack and the existing evidence foundation. It implements a small rule vocabulary: activity membership, reviewed text patterns, derived signals, model conditions, additive traits, model-specific traits, and first-matching decisive rules. That vocabulary was sufficient to reproduce Sacramento without a Sacramento branch.

The proof deliberately did not make the evaluator a production recommendation engine. It is under `scripts/lib`, is imported only by proof QA, and has no activation or runtime wiring. This leaves the current certified implementation as the sole customer path.

## What Could Disappear

If a shared Market Pack path eventually became canonical after another proof and a controlled migration:

- the 70-line Sacramento Requirement adapter could disappear;
- the 67-line Sacramento composition module could disappear;
- Sacramento-specific universe construction and dispatch branches in readiness could become one shared pack lookup and projection;
- duplicated adapter/composer unit assertions could become contract-driven fixtures; and
- future fixes to intent resolution, ordering, mixed handling, or evidence projection could be tested once rather than repeated per market.

The immediate, defensible reduction is therefore 137 lines across two Sacramento-only modules, plus roughly 25–40 lines of named readiness branching once the runtime is safely generalized. The proof adds 119 lines of shared evaluator code; that cost is paid once and should replace comparable modules across markets only after further validation. The value is fewer independent implementations, not line-count reduction by itself.

Approximately 35–45 Sacramento-specific decisions moved from executable conditions into governed data: aliases and ownership, activity groups, reviewed text triggers, exclusion patterns, conflict groups, fit bands, derived signals, trait mappings, decisive rules, and bounded explanations.

## What Remains Market-Specific

- municipality and market ownership;
- canonical candidate geography and component relationships;
- reviewed evidence and provenance;
- candidate distinctions, strengths, tradeoffs, and operating traits;
- which Requirement signals those reviewed traits can support;
- true market-specific abstention boundaries and their scope explanations;
- representative examples and their non-availability semantics;
- certification fixtures and human review; and
- explicit activation approval.

These are not abstraction failures. They are the market knowledge Rofo must govern.

## Future Market Input Checklist

For another Industrial/Flex market, a researcher/operator would need to:

1. Define the supported municipalities, market aliases, and explicit exclusions.
2. Identify the bounded peer commercial geographies and reconcile components with parents.
3. Provide reviewed evidence for what distinguishes each peer.
4. Record Industrial, Flex, and mixed operating traits that the evidence actually supports.
5. Identify canonical Requirement activities or stated needs that activate those traits.
6. Identify needs the geography evidence cannot resolve and must cause investigation or abstention.
7. Supply reviewed representative environments or properties with explicit non-availability semantics.
8. Preserve source provenance, review status, and evidence boundaries.
9. Author one-peer, multi-peer, mixed, and investigate certification cases.
10. Pass customer-language, Location Brief, municipality, evidence, default-deny, and activation review.

With a validated shared evaluator, coding should be limited to registering the governed pack/foundation and correcting genuinely new shared-contract needs. Research, evidence review, calibration, fixtures, and certification remain substantial work.

## Estimated Expansion Burden

The proof suggests that the adapter/composition portion of future market development could fall by roughly **half to two-thirds**: instead of authoring and debugging two market-specific modules plus readiness branches, the team would supply governed configuration and fixtures. Total market work would fall less—approximately **25–40%**—because evidence collection, geography reconciliation, provenance, calibration, customer-language review, and certification are still the hard and necessary parts.

QA becomes simpler in structure, not smaller in responsibility. One generic contract suite can enforce membership, abstention, ordering, evidence projection, and Location Brief invariants, while each market still needs meaningful reference cases and default-deny activation checks.

## Go / No-Go

**GO for the Market Pack direction; no-go for immediate production migration.** Sacramento demonstrates that market knowledge can replace most Sacramento-specific adapter/composition software without changing behavior. It does not yet demonstrate that the rule vocabulary covers a structurally different market or that current runtime branching can be removed safely.

## Recommended Next Step

Run one more default-off equivalence proof using **Phoenix Industrial/Flex**. Reuse this exact pack contract and shared evaluator first; extend the shared rule vocabulary only if Phoenix exposes a genuinely reusable need. If both controls pass, migrate Sacramento and Phoenix through a separately reviewed runtime change with default-deny activation unchanged, then use the model for the next new Industrial/Flex market.

Do not migrate all markets, introduce a generic recommendation rewrite, redesign evidence, or add a new market during that proof.
