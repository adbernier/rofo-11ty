# San Francisco Industrial/Flex Evidence Foundation

Status: Evidence approved for future public surfacing; public pages remain deferred

## Evidence outcome

Bayview Industrial and Central Waterfront each have four Commercial Market Evidence records. The collections optimize for distinct operating models rather than building count.

### Bayview Industrial

- The SF Market: official-operator evidence for wholesale food, distribution, overnight receiving, pickup, and delivery infrastructure.
- Bayview Industrial Triangle: official Planning evidence for protected everyday PDR, contractor, repair, production, and service-industrial fabric.
- SF Gateway: official Planning evidence for a **proposed** modern multistory PDR format. It is development evidence, not delivered inventory or a Building Profile.
- Piers 80–96: official Port evidence for specialized maritime, cargo, rail, bulk-material, vehicle-processing, and heavy-commercial context. It is not conventional recommendation inventory.

No Bayview Building Brief was added. The source standard supports the campus and operating environments, but not enough stable property-level facts for a conventional representative building without further address-level research.

### Central Waterfront

- American Industrial Center: existing Dogpatch-owned Building Profile referenced as shared transition evidence. The canonical building record is not duplicated or reassigned.
- Pier 70 Building 12: existing Dogpatch-owned Building Profile referenced as industrial-heritage and maker-transition evidence. Pier 70 does not represent the whole district.
- Central Waterfront Core PDR: official Planning evidence for production, fabrication, repair, arts, prototyping, and practical flex south of mixed-use Dogpatch.
- Central Waterfront maritime-support edge: official Planning and Port evidence for specialized ship-repair, fabrication, storage, and maritime-support context.

## Ownership decisions

Dogpatch continues to own American Industrial Center and the detailed Pier 70 building collection. Central Waterfront consumes those two records as contextual shared evidence and owns the protected core PDR and maritime-support interpretation. This preserves URLs, building identity, and existing Dogpatch public content.

Showplace Square remains the intended knowledge owner for the overlapping Showplace Square / Design District evidence geography. This sprint makes no destructive CME, building, or URL migration.

## Evidence limitations

Evidence records explain operating patterns. They do not prove current availability, tenancy, loading, power, parking, yard access, permitted use, environmental condition, building systems, or suite condition. Those questions remain property-specific.

## Publishing readiness

- Bayview Industrial district surface: **Ready with minor limitations.** The operating model is well evidenced, but conventional existing-building examples remain thin and should be described honestly.
- Central Waterfront district surface: **Ready with minor limitations.** The district has strong official geography evidence and two contextual Building Profiles, but the public page must preserve Dogpatch ownership and avoid letting Pier 70 dominate.
- Industrial/Flex property-type decision hub: **Ready with minor limitations.** The decision distinctions are supported; the first public sprint should surface Bayview, Central Waterfront, Dogpatch, and Showplace roles without adding recommendation rankings or thin building claims.

## Recommendation QA readiness

The evidence supports future scenarios for city-serving warehouse/distribution, food wholesale, contractor/service industrial, urban production/prototyping, adaptive office-production, showroom/design trade, and regional-logistics comparison. This foundation does not change the SF Office model or introduce an Industrial/Flex ranking model.
