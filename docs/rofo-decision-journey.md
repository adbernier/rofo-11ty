# Rofo Decision Journey

Rofo helps businesses make better commercial location decisions.

The product is evolving from a commercial real estate listings website into a decision platform that combines location intelligence, education, execution support, and trusted local brokerage.

The organizing principle is simple:

1. Where should I look?
2. What should I expect?
3. How do I get the deal done?

Every page, module, workflow, and partnership should help answer one of these questions.

## Mission

Rofo's mission is to help businesses understand commercial geography, evaluate the right markets, and move confidently from location strategy to a completed commercial real estate process.

This means Rofo should not only show available space. Rofo should help a business understand:

- which cities, districts, corridors, and commercial areas deserve attention
- why one location may fit better than another
- what tradeoffs matter before touring buildings
- what leasing process, budget, timing, and negotiation issues to expect
- when to involve a local broker or expert
- how to convert a Location Brief into a practical execution path

## North Star

The North Star is:

A business can start anywhere on Rofo and end with a clear, explainable Location Brief and a practical path to execution.

The Location Brief is the product artifact that connects the full journey:

- it captures the customer's requirements
- it reflects Rofo's location intelligence
- it explains tradeoffs
- it prepares expert review
- it supports broker handoff
- it can evolve into collaboration, CRM, PDF, and referral workflows

## The Three Customer Questions

### 1. Where should I look?

This is the Location Intelligence pillar.

The customer is trying to decide:

- which city, district, corridor, or submarket should be considered
- whether a preferred location is actually a fit
- which nearby markets should be compared
- which locations are better for office, industrial, flex, medical, retail, or other use cases
- what the best starting market path should be

Primary product capabilities:

- Rofo Compass
- Commercial Location Knowledge Graph
- recommendation resolver
- explainability layer
- `/find-locations/`
- `/recommendations/`
- Location Brief
- district pages
- comparison pages
- Rofo Insights
- representative buildings
- market snapshots

Primary CTA:

Find My Best Locations

### 2. What should I expect?

This is the Education pillar.

The customer is trying to understand:

- how commercial leasing works
- what lease structures mean
- how budget and occupancy cost should be evaluated
- what timing is realistic
- what tenant improvements are
- how broker relationships work
- what a landlord or broker will ask
- what information is needed before moving forward

Primary product capabilities:

- leasing guides
- market guides
- space-type pages
- budget modules
- timeline modules
- market snapshots
- educational callouts
- Rofo Insights where the content explains a decision pattern

Primary CTA:

Understand the Process

Secondary CTA:

Find My Best Locations

### 3. How do I get the deal done?

This is the Execution pillar.

The customer is trying to move from strategy to action:

- review live availability
- validate the Location Brief with a local expert
- evaluate specific buildings
- compare listings
- understand deal terms
- connect with a qualified broker
- manage broker referral and next steps

Primary product capabilities:

- expert review request
- lead dashboard
- broker partner directory
- referral workflow
- representative building pages
- available commercial space pages
- OfficeFinder partner routing
- future partner portal
- future Location Brief collaboration

Primary CTA:

Request Expert Review

## Product Capabilities by Stage

### Awareness

The customer has a general need but has not defined the decision.

Capabilities:

- homepage
- city pages
- space-type pages
- high-level leasing guides
- introductory Rofo Insights
- "How Rofo Works"

Success metric:

The customer understands that Rofo helps with commercial location decisions, not only listings.

### Exploration

The customer is comparing areas and trying to learn.

Capabilities:

- district pages
- comparison pages
- market guides
- market snapshots
- representative buildings
- Insight hubs
- related Insight modules

Success metric:

The customer moves from browsing to a clearer location question.

### Recommendation

The customer is ready to personalize the decision.

Capabilities:

- recommendation prompt card
- `/find-locations/`
- location intent
- recommendation resolver
- explainability layer
- `/recommendations/`
- Location Brief

Success metric:

The customer receives a credible Location Brief that explains where to start and why.

### Validation

The customer wants human review and real market context.

Capabilities:

- expert review request
- canonical public Location Brief
- lead dashboard
- broker partner matching
- OfficeFinder routing where appropriate

Success metric:

The Location Brief becomes operationally visible and ready for expert action.

### Execution

The customer needs broker support, tours, pricing, terms, and deal guidance.

Capabilities:

- broker partner directory
- referral object
- referral workflow
- broker-facing opportunity page
- contact reveal after acceptance
- future partner portal
- future CRM and collaboration

Success metric:

A qualified local expert accepts responsibility for helping the customer move forward.

## Future Roadmap Alignment

Future work should be evaluated against the three questions.

### Where should I look?

Priority work:

- expand Compass Ready metros
- enrich district and comparison coverage
- improve recommendation explainability
- deepen Location Brief personalization
- connect Insights to Compass relationships

### What should I expect?

Priority work:

- build universal leasing education
- create space-type-specific education paths
- add budget and timing calculators or explainers
- connect leasing concepts into Location Brief preparation

### How do I get the deal done?

Priority work:

- mature broker partner workflow
- build partner portal
- track referral lifecycle
- add customer collaboration around Location Briefs
- connect expert review to available listings, comps, incentives, and next steps

## Operating Principle

Rofo should not ask customers to choose between content, search, and experts.

The correct experience is a decision journey:

Learn enough to ask the right question.

Generate a Location Brief.

Validate with an expert.

Move toward the deal.
