# Rofo Product Vision

## Mission

Rofo exists to help businesses make better commercial location decisions.

Finding available space is only one part of the problem.

The harder problem is determining where a business should be.

Rofo exists to help businesses answer that question.

## The Core Insight

Businesses are not looking for a Location Brief.

Businesses are trying to solve a problem.

They want to:

- find the right location
- compare markets
- understand tradeoffs
- make a confident decision

The Location Brief is the product Rofo creates while helping solve that problem.

The Search Profile is intentionally short.

The Location Brief grows over time.

## The Commercial Location Knowledge Graph

The Commercial Location Knowledge Graph is the intellectual property behind Rofo.

It is not simply geography.

It captures commercial location knowledge.

Every location should eventually include:

- geographic relationships
- representative buildings
- comparison relationships
- space-type fit
- business attributes
- retail attributes
- industrial attributes
- best-for guidance
- tradeoffs
- questions to validate
- recommendation relationships

The graph stores broker knowledge.

The recommendation engine queries this knowledge.

## The Location Brief

The Location Brief is the primary product produced by Rofo.

It is the canonical object in the platform.

Everything enriches the Location Brief.

Everything consumes the Location Brief.

It should eventually include:

- Search Profile
- Recommended Market Path
- Business Priorities
- Why these markets
- Representative Buildings
- Tradeoffs
- Questions to Explore
- User Notes
- Current Location (future)
- Why Moving (future)
- Broker Notes (future)
- Tour History (future)
- Final Decision (future)

The Location Brief should become something companies save, revisit, share, and discuss internally.

## Recommendations

Recommendations are not the final product.

Recommendations are one section of the Location Brief.

The goal is not to provide the "perfect answer."

The goal is to provide an intelligent starting point and explain why.

## Broker Experience

Brokers should not receive leads.

They should receive Location Briefs.

The Location Brief should dramatically reduce discovery time.

The broker should immediately understand:

- business requirements
- recommended market path
- user priorities
- questions still to explore

Long term, brokers should review the Location Brief directly on Rofo.

## OfficeFinder

OfficeFinder is an important distribution partner.

However, the product should never depend on OfficeFinder.

The Location Brief should remain the canonical object.

Today:

Rofo → OfficeFinder → Broker

Long term:

Rofo → Location Brief → Broker Network

OfficeFinder becomes one possible distribution channel.

## Product Philosophy

Every future feature should answer one question:

Does this make the Location Brief more valuable?

If not, reconsider whether it belongs.

## Long-Term Vision

Rofo should become the trusted advisor for commercial location decisions.

The Commercial Location Knowledge Graph powers those decisions.

The Location Brief documents those decisions.

Together they create a product that becomes more valuable every time a metro is expanded and every time broker knowledge is added.

Rofo is not trying to build a better listing website.

Rofo is building commercial location intelligence.
