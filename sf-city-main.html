Local guidance

How to think about leasing space in San Francisco, CA

Finding the right space comes down to balancing location, budget, layout, and timing. In San Francisco, it helps to start with your use case, then compare neighborhoods, accessibility, and total occupancy cost.

Start with your use

Office, retail, industrial, and flex users all have different needs. Clarify team size, customer access, parking, and workflow before touring.

Budget beyond base rent

Total cost often includes CAM/NNN, utilities, deposits, insurance, and improvements—not just advertised rent.

Plan your timing

Searching, touring, negotiation, and move-in can take longer than expected— especially if buildout or permits are involved.

Compare neighborhoods

The right block often matters more than the building. Look at foot traffic, access, nearby businesses, and how the area fits your brand.
