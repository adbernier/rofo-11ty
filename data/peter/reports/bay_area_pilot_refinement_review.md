- Downtown Oakland
- Uptown Oakland

Secondary refinement-only districts:

- South San Francisco Biotech Corridor

## Downtown Oakland

### Boundary and Assignment Refinement

Downtown Oakland has the cleanest pilot assignment profile: 804 raw buildings, 1,725 historical listing rows, 702 high-confidence assignments, and 102 medium-confidence assignments.

Refinement decision:

- Keep Downtown Oakland centered on Broadway, City Center, Frank H. Ogawa Plaza, 12th-16th Street civic/professional office fabric, and BART-oriented downtown blocks.
- Exclude `2201 Broadway` from Downtown Oakland representative examples because current public page data treats it as Uptown Oakland.
- Do not broaden the page into all of central Oakland or Lake Merritt.

### Differentiated Identity

Downtown Oakland should read as the East Bay's institutional downtown core. It is formal, civic, BART-oriented, and professional-service oriented.

Public positioning:

> East Bay institutional business core with BART access, civic adjacency, and professional office depth.

### Nearby Comparisons

- Uptown Oakland: more mixed-use and smaller-company oriented, with stronger Uptown arts and retail context.
- Jack London Square: more waterfront and warehouse-adjacent, with service-commercial and adaptive texture.
- Old Oakland: smaller-scale historic blocks just west of the formal downtown core.
- San Francisco Financial District: more traditional regional CBD setting across the bay.

### Representative Examples

Approved for pilot:

- `1333 Broadway`
- `505 14th St`
- `300 Frank H Ogawa Plz`
- `1440 Broadway`

Quality note:

The set is office-heavy, which matches the district identity. Add one historic or street-level downtown example in a later pass if a reliable public building page is available.

### Public Editorial Direction

Use:

- civic institutions
- BART access
- Broadway office corridor
- professional services
- East Bay alternative to San Francisco CBD

Avoid:

- generic downtown copy
- current availability claims
- source/provenance language
- treating representative buildings as intelligence

## Uptown Oakland

### Boundary and Assignment Refinement

Uptown Oakland has strong raw support but needs a more cautious launch posture: 1,093 raw buildings, 1,252 historical listing rows, 461 high-confidence assignments, and 632 medium-confidence assignments.

Refinement decision:

- Keep Uptown framed around Broadway, Franklin, Webster, 19th St BART, Lake Merritt edge, arts/food context, and mixed-use commercial blocks.
- Treat Downtown-adjacent and Lake Merritt edge examples carefully.
- Do not use `2201 Broadway` in the pilot representative set until the Downtown/Uptown conflict is resolved.

### Differentiated Identity

Uptown Oakland should not read like a second Downtown Oakland page. It should feel more mixed-use, smaller-company oriented, arts/food adjacent, and less civic/formal.

Public positioning:

> Mixed-use Oakland office district for smaller-company, arts-adjacent, and transit-oriented teams.

### Nearby Comparisons

- Downtown Oakland: more formal, civic, and traditional office-core oriented.
- Jack London Square: more waterfront and service-commercial, with warehouse-adjacent texture.
- Temescal: more neighborhood retail and small-business oriented north of Uptown.
- Berkeley: more university-adjacent and North East Bay oriented.

### Representative Examples

Approved for pilot:

- `1 Kaiser Plz`
- `2101 Webster St`
- `1970 Broadway`
- `415 20th St`

Quality note:

The approved set is still office-heavy. It is acceptable for pilot launch preparation, but the next refinement should add one stronger mixed-use, arts-adjacent, or street-level support example.

### Public Editorial Direction

Use:

- mixed-use counterpart to Downtown Oakland
- arts venues, food, apartments, BART
- Lake Merritt-adjacent commercial blocks
- smaller and mid-sized office users

Avoid:

- making Uptown a second CBD
- overclaiming adaptive-reuse identity without examples
- hiding the boundary caution in internal review

## Downtown Palo Alto

### Boundary and Assignment Refinement

Downtown Palo Alto is the strongest new entity candidate in the pilot: 135 raw buildings, 373 historical listing rows, 125 high-confidence assignments, and 10 medium-confidence assignments.

Refinement decision:

- Keep the page centered on University Avenue, Hamilton Avenue, Lytton Avenue, downtown retail/restaurant support, and Caltrain-adjacent office fabric.
- Keep Town & Country Village as context, not a primary representative example.
- Keep California Avenue as a nearby comparison corridor.
- Keep Stanford Research Park and campus/R&D examples out of the Downtown Palo Alto page.

### Differentiated Identity

Downtown Palo Alto should read as premium and walkable without becoming hype language. The product story is client-facing downtown presence, startup/professional routines, low-rise office fabric, retail support, and Caltrain access.

Public positioning:

> Walkable Peninsula downtown for startup, professional service, and client-facing office users.

### Nearby Comparisons

- Mountain View / Castro-Whisman: more R&D and startup-corridor oriented, with broader Castro and Whisman context.
- Redwood City Downtown: more civic and mid-Peninsula downtown oriented, with a larger entertainment and Caltrain-adjacent core.
- Menlo Park: nearby affluent Peninsula alternative with smaller downtown and business-park context.
- California Avenue: more local and secondary commercial corridor compared with the University Avenue downtown core.

### Representative Examples

Approved for pilot:

- `530 Lytton Ave`
- `228 Hamilton Ave`
- `200-228 Hamilton Ave`
- `400 Hamilton Ave`

Quality note:

The set includes office plus retail-supported downtown fabric. The image should carry more of the walkable downtown character.

### Public Editorial Direction

Use:

- compact Peninsula business district
- low-rise office buildings
- restaurants, retail, and Caltrain access
- startup/professional-service routines
- client-facing address

Avoid:

- Silicon Valley hype
- generic "premier" phrasing
- Stanford campus or Research Park conflation
- pricing/current availability claims from historical data

## Oakland Pair QA

Downtown Oakland and Uptown Oakland must be visibly distinct:

- Downtown Oakland: institutional, civic, BART, formal office core, Broadway/City Center, public-sector and professional-service adjacency.
- Uptown Oakland: mixed-use, arts/food, Lake Merritt edge, Broadway/Franklin/Webster, smaller-company fit, less formal than Downtown.

Do not reuse the same comparison copy across the pair. Each should explain why a user would compare one with the other.
