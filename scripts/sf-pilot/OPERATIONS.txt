Rofo SF tenant pilot — controlled cohort operations

Purpose
Here are the buildings we'd explore first. Keep a few meaningfully different,
source-backed options and the approved tenant voice. Shared memberships, serviced
private offices, managed offices and traditional leases remain distinct. Fewer than
three supported options is preferable to invented matches. No vacancy, quote,
photography, tour confirmation or comprehensive landlord coverage is required.

Explicit invitation and identity
1. The participant completes the EXISTING SF office interview and shares their
   Location Brief reference with the authorized Rofo staff member. This first,
   not-yet-invited interview retains ordinary Rofo behavior, including its current
   draft behavior. No new questionnaire or brokerage referral is required to invite.
2. Staff verifies the intended participant and exact delivery mailbox through the
   approved business communication channel. In the authenticated staff workspace,
   enter that SF office Brief reference/email and explicitly check the invitation
   review box. The server binds invitation to that Brief and mailbox.
3. "Prepare personal invitation" creates a staff-only invitation preview; it sends
   NOTHING. Deliver its private one-use link ONLY to the bound mailbox using the
   separately approved manual email process. Do not post links in chat, logs, shared
   spreadsheets or research tools. Do not click a real participant's invitation in
   staff preview: it would consume their personal capability. Local synthetic review
   may deliberately open its own fixture link.
4. The participant deliberately opens the mailbox link. GET/scanners do not consume
   it; the confirmation POST verifies the one-use24h capability and creates a
   scoped browser invitation session. Typing an invited email or owning the ordinary
   Brief alone never admits anyone. Intake pre-fills the verified mailbox read-only;
   server rejects a different email. Invite is bound to one Brief, not every search
   made by that mailbox or browser. Participant can then provide missing workspace
   details and separate research/email permission.
5. Pilot identity is possession of a private link delivered to the invited mailbox,
   not a full account or proof of legal business identity. Trusted staff controls
   delivery; do not forward or share invitation links. Invitation eligibility lasts
   until explicit revocation; individual links expire24h and sessions30days. A new
   device needs another invitation link before intake, or a shortlist/recovery link
   after enrollment. Invited-browser SF drafts preserve back/reload context.
6. Reissue via the same staff form with the SAME Brief/mailbox; pending older invite
   links are revoked. Already enrolled searches cannot be transferred to another
   participant/mailbox. For a genuinely different participant use a new Brief.
7. "Revoke this pilot invitation" immediately denies invitation/intake, private
   access, new recovery, feedback/research and consent. It revokes related grants,
   holds pending inquiry permissions, suppresses pending/failed mail and increments
   search generation. It does not recall information or email already handed off.
   Sign-out revokes that browser's invitation session as well as its search session.

Research and shortlist review
- Queue owner checks new research, updates, feedback and recovery daily at the
  agreed internal cadence. Research stays inside Rofo. It never enrolls a lead in
  OfficeFinder or authorizes an external introduction.
- Use the canonical Brief and scoped workspace extension. Ask only for missing
  information. Do not recreate or silently widen district/space-type requirements.
- Prepare assembles an UNPUBLISHED draft from reviewed operator evidence. Human
  review must check source/date/address, product type, roster vs onsite count,
  privacy, capacity/eligibility, price unit/term/fees and tenant-specific reasons.
  AI assistance here means draft organization from approved attributable evidence;
  no personal details are sent to a new AI provider. New landlord/broker research
  is separate enrichment and is not a release requirement.
- Exclude poor options; never pad to3–5. Publish only after checking the exact
  rendered version. The content hash/revision binds that review. Requirement edits
  hide stale cards and hold old permissions until fresh review/publication.
- Prepare shortlist email then preview it. Deliver only current reviewed messages
  through the approved configured transport. Provider acceptance is not inbox
  delivery. Mock mode sends nothing; disabled mode sends nothing. Keep the private
  claim link out of analytics and third-party click tracking.

Recovery and delivery problems
- Recovery responses are identical for matching/missing/revoked searches. Only an
  active invitation with matching reference/mailbox queues a job. It does not send
  automatically. Staff reviews the saved recipient and prepares/delivers a fresh
  current link. Delivery/timing are not guaranteed. Disabled-send wording says so.
- Before enrollment or if the invitation link expires, confirm the SAME intended
  mailbox through the approved channel and prepare another invitation. Never infer
  identity from a new typed email or transfer another participant's saved search.
- Check the daily mail queue for pending/failed/unknown/sending jobs. Failed BEFORE
  provider acceptance may be retried. An unknown/sending outcome must be reconciled
  against actual provider evidence first; record receipt/outcome, do not blindly
  retry an old message. Do not equate provider acceptance with delivery/read.
- Expired/consumed/revoked links cannot render again. Prepare a new current recovery
  job when appropriate. Report suspected disclosure; revoke access/invitation and
  preserve the incident/send history. Never put credentials in a support report.

Interest and external inquiries
- I'm interested offers suitable-space availability, pricing/details or exploring a
  tour. This creates an internal request, not a booking or outside message.
- Staff identifies the actual appropriate recipient and explicitly reviews their
  address. Tenant then sees exact recipient, purpose and fields and gives fresh,
  unchecked permission. Research permission and staff review are NOT inquiry consent.
- Share only the current authorized payload through an independently approved manual
  outreach process. This application has no outbound operator/broker adapter. Record
  actual outcome/receipt or mark unknown. Do not claim availability/tour confirmation.
- Withdrawals stop pending handling. New recipient, purpose, payload, requirement or
  shortlist version requires fresh permission. Revocation cannot undo information
  already shared; notify the owner and handle that separately.
